MFR-Tannhauser - free late-'90s art-punk guitar rig by Music For Robots
https://musicforrobots.com
Source code: https://github.com/music-for-robots/MFR-Tannhauser

INSTALLING (zip version)
  Copy the MFR-Tannhauser.vst3 folder into:
      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  Then rescan plugins in your DAW (in FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins
  > Find plugins). MFR-Tannhauser.exe is a standalone version: pick your
  audio interface under Options, and plug your guitar in.

WHAT IT DOES
  Two channels for the quiet parts and the loud parts:
  CLEAN is a solid-state combo with spring reverb into a 1x12,
  DIRTY is a British 50 W head into a 4x12. Both go through a close
  mic, a room mic and 2-inch tape, and DOUBLE spreads the guitar into
  a hard-panned double-track. Tuned for drop D, works in anything.

  CHANNEL Click CLEAN or DIRTY. Each channel keeps its own knobs.
  CLEAN   Volume, Bass, Mid, Treble, Spring, BRIGHT.
  DIRTY   Preamp (gain), Bass, Middle, Treble, Presence, Master.
          BOOST puts a mid-hump boost pedal in front: tighter, more gain.
  IN/OUT  Gate (all the way down = off; the light shows it open) and
          Output.
  STUDIO  Room: close mic to room mic. Tape: how hard the tape is hit.
          Double: 0 = one guitar in the middle, 10 = two takes hard
          left and right.
  INIT resets every control.

  Knobs: double-click or Alt-click resets to the default.
         Ctrl- or Shift-drag gives fine control.
         Click the value under a knob to type in a number.
